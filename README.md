
# 📊 Marketing Performance Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Project

This **Power BI dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of **Marketing Performance** across multiple channels and regions.  
It focuses on tracking **Revenue, Cost, ROI, CTR, and Conversions** to help marketing teams and decision-makers understand campaign effectiveness and optimize marketing strategies.

The dashboard combines **visual KPIs, interactive filters, and channel-wise analytics** to deliver clear insights into which platforms and regions drive the most value.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To analyze and visualize **marketing performance metrics** across multiple channels — such as **Email, Google Ads, Social Media, and SMS** — and regions (North, South, East, West), in order to:

- Identify top-performing marketing channels  
- Understand ROI and cost-effectiveness  
- Evaluate regional sales and spending patterns  
- Improve conversion rates and marketing efficiency

## ❓ Questions Answered (KPIs Used)

### **Overall Marketing Dashboard**

- 💰 **Total Revenue** – What is the total revenue generated from all campaigns?  
- 💸 **Total Cost** – What is the total marketing spend?  
- 🖱️ **Total Clicks** – How many users interacted with campaigns?  
- 🎯 **Total Conversions** – How many conversions were achieved?  
- 📈 **CTR (%)** – What is the average click-through rate?  
- 🔁 **ROI (%)** – What is the overall return on investment?  
- 🧮 **Conversion Rate (%)** – What percentage of clicks turned into conversions?  

---

### **Channel Performance Dashboard**

- 📊 **Revenue by Channel** – Which marketing channels generated the most revenue?  
- 📬 **ROI by Channel** – Which platforms provided the best ROI?  
- 🔄 **Conversions by Channel** – How did conversions vary across channels?  
- 💵 **Cost vs. Revenue** – Which channels were most cost-efficient?  

---

### **Regional Performance Dashboard**

- 🌍 **Revenue and Spend by Region** – Which regions contributed the most to revenue?  
- 🧭 **Regional ROI** – Where is marketing spend producing the highest returns?  
- 📊 **Category-wise Revenue** – How did product categories perform in each region (Beauty, Electronics, Fashion, Groceries, Home Decor)?

## 🔍 Project Insights

### **Marketing Performance Insights**
- 💰 **Total Revenue:** 28M  
- 💸 **Total Cost:** 17M  
- 🎯 **Total Conversions:** 257K  
- 🔁 **Average ROI:** 135.65%  
- 📈 **Average CTR:** 14.50%  
- 📊 **Average Conversion Rate:** 32.55%  

---

### **Channel Insights**
- 🧩 **Email** and **Google Ads** generated the highest ROI and conversions.  
- 📱 **Social Media** showed consistent engagement with strong click-through rates.  
- 💬 **SMS Marketing** delivered steady conversions at moderate cost.  

---

### **Regional Insights**
- 🧭 **South Region** achieved the **highest revenue (₹8.1M)** compared to other regions.  
- 🌍 **North Region** followed closely with a balanced cost-to-revenue ratio.  
- 🗺️ **Beauty and Fashion** categories contributed the most across all regions.
